## Filing and integration recommendation

Use the [official complaint portal](https://cie.odpc.go.ke/Complaint) as a consumer-controlled handoff. The public form was observed to request identification, contact and complaint details, evidence, remedies and email verification; an on-behalf option and authority upload are displayed. Do not type the consumer's identity or email code on their behalf, relay a code to staff, scrape around portal controls, or mark a click as a filing. No test complaint was submitted.

No supported public machine-to-machine filing interface was verified in the official portal/CIE materials searched. This is an absence of verification, not proof that ODPC has no internal integration. Alternatives are self-filing with a versioned pack, a confirmed accessible channel, or a separately authorised human representative if lawful and accepted. Any future API needs written channel approval, documented authentication and receipts, security/transfer review, and a new owner decision.

## Questions to resolve before real evidence or charging

Send no messages as part of this audit. These are an agenda for a later authorised engagement:

1. **Kenyan advocate:** give written service-boundary opinion using actual template letters, pack sample, proposed prices and support scripts; review professional indemnity/cyber liability and independent referrals.
2. **ODPC/counsel:** confirm current identity requirements by channel, representative mandates/e-signatures, anonymous/minor/accessibility routes and current official contact points.
3. **Counsel:** reconcile regulation, manual, charter and case-specific directions; approve deadline calculations, appeal and enforcement procedures.
4. **Privacy lead/counsel:** decide registration category, DPIA submission/consultation, lawful bases, transfers, contracts and record retention.
5. **Research owner:** retrieve authenticated determinations/current consolidations and verify later appeals/stays before publishing case examples. The blocked official2023 index returned unexpected unrelated content in one retrieval; treat source health as untrusted, without asserting the site was compromised.

No generic ISO certification requirement for this platform was established by the sources inspected. Registration, professional authorisation, security assurance and ISO certification are different questions; do not sell any as a substitute for the others.

DPO appointment also needs a specific assessment under DPA section24; appoint a named accountable privacy lead now and ask counsel whether the actual processing requires a designated DPO. Do not infer an exemption or a universal appointment mandate from company size alone.
