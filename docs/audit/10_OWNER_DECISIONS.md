## Approval sequence

1. Review executive assessment and visual boards; select design/service direction D01/D02/D04.
2. Approve a **phase0 validation scope and budget**, if desired, identifying who will obtain legal/privacy/process confirmations. This is separate work; the audit itself did not contact counsel, ODPC or users.
3. Record resolutions to D03/D05/D07/D09 and attach signed-off documents. Resolve verified-source gaps before encoding rules.
4. Only then issue a specific development instruction naming phase1, accepted feature/design IDs, budget and release gates. Later phases require subsequent approval.

Suggested approval record fields: decision ID, chosen option, rationale, owner/date, evidence, conditions, affected files/features, budget, expiry/review date. Leave status “pending” until the owner explicitly decides. A design preference does not approve data processing or a production release.
