## How to maintain this register

Before implementing a legal rule, attach an authenticated source copy or stable official URL, provision, effective version, retrieval date, reviewer and review expiry. For a specific case, attach the actual notice/order and preserve any amendments. A website domain, a generated summary or a search snippet alone cannot verify legal outcome, mailbox ownership or a binding deadline.

Provider pricing and features are point-in-time facts. A changelog fetch was unsuccessful; current official documentation was used instead. No production SDK version was selected or installed, and no backend compatibility claim is made.
