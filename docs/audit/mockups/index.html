Proposed end-to-end workflow

A useful default, with room for exceptions.

01 / INTAKEEvidence or description, privacy notice and verified contact.
02 / TRIAGEReview gaps and attribution. Urgent cases can take an early official route.
03 / AUTHORISEExact company, request and attachments. Nothing implied.
04 / RESPONDTrack delivery, reply and the consumer's chosen next step.
05 / OPTIONAL ODPCReview a versioned pack. Consumer files. Verify official records.
