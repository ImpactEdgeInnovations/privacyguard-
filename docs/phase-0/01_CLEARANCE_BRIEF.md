# Counsel and privacy lead brief

**Source follow-up checked 10 November 2026.** This supplement records new evidence against the [audit legal findings](../audit/03_LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY.md). Application-specific clearance remains pending.

## Sources recovered

| Topic | What the texts say | Product decision |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Regulation 4(4) says seven days from receipt. The manual §2.8 and the 2024/25 charter use seven working days. | Keep an acknowledgement as a distinct event supported by correspondence. Counsel should confirm applicable counting and whether newer operational guidance exists. [Regulations](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/264/eng%402022-12-31/source.pdf), [manual](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Complaints-Management-Manual-1.pdf), [charter](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ODPC-Service-Charter-English-2025.pdf) |
| Respondent timing | The manual §3.2 describes a preliminary inquiry sequence of 14 days and two seven-day reminders. Regulation 11(1) provides 21 days in a formal post-admission DPC 3 notice. | Treat these as separately sourced stages. Ask how they are used in practice and which date starts each clock. Preserve the actual notice and date stated on it. [Manual](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Complaints-Management-Manual-1.pdf), [regulations](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/264/eng%402022-12-31/source.pdf) |
| Submission and evidence | Regulation 4 permits multiple channels and free lodging. The public portal exposes identity and verification fields. The manual §4.2 calls for certified supporting copies. | Keep the direct official route visible. Confirm when certified copies are required and what alternatives apply to users unable to use the portal. [Regulations](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/264/eng%402022-12-31/source.pdf), [portal](https://cie.odpc.go.ke/Complaint), [manual](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Complaints-Management-Manual-1.pdf) |
| DPIA | Section 31 provides for prior assessment for likely high risk, consultation where the assessment indicates high risk, and report submission 60 days before processing. The regulator guidance describes submission and assessment contents. | Record applicability, decision rationale, receipt and consultation outcome as applicable. Schedule real processing against that evidence. [Act §31](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng%402022-12-31/source), [ODPC DPIA guidance](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/ODPC-Guidance-Note-on-Data-Protection-Impact-Assessment-1.pdf) |

The manual and charter were inspected through extracted official text. The charter is labelled 2024/25. Current operating practice, portal field enforcement and the audit's outstanding case authentication questions still need separate verification.
