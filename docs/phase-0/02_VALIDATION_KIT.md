# Moderated validation kit

Use the [standalone boards](../audit/mockups/index.html) with fictional material. Start each exercise by describing the goal and asking participants to point to their next action and explain what they expect. Capture their first answer before prompting. Label screen limitations as board limitations in the notes.

Use the [English cards](intake-test-cards.html) and [draft Kiswahili cards](intake-test-cards-sw.html) for V01, V05 and V06. Have a fluent reviewer compare the action, status and privacy language across both versions before Kiswahili-first sessions. The rest of the facilitator script and the audit boards still need equivalent Kiswahili prompts. See the [session plan and budget](06_SESSION_PLAN_AND_BUDGET.md).

Target 12–15 adults for usability, including Kiswahili-first users, people with lower digital literacy and people using small phones. Use a fluent facilitator and have a native reviewer check equivalent prompts. Recruit through an owner-approved route. Agree handling of recruitment contact details before scheduling. Use participant codes, redact notes and use synthetic cases throughout. Run price interviews separately or record prior exposure to the usability session.

### Paper prompts for V01

| Card | Participant-facing text |
|---|---|
| A. Evidence first | **What happened?** Choose a screenshot or describe the call or message. Leave out details about other people. |
| B. Goal first | **What would you like to happen?** Stop the marketing · Understand how the company got my details · I'm still deciding. Next, choose a screenshot or describe what happened. |
| Shared description | **Tell us about the call or message.** Describe what happened. Add the sender and date if you know them. If the date is uncertain, choose “I'm not sure”. |
| Shared review | **Review your report.** Check the description, date and goal. Verify your contact to save the report to your private account. Submitting the report asks PrivacyGuard to review it. |
| Shared receipt | **We received your report.** You can find it in My cases. PrivacyGuard is reviewing the information you provided. |

In variant A, show the same goal choices after the description. Keep choice text, verification and receipt constant between versions. Use the cards as text prompts or display them on a phone during moderation.

### Facilitator rehearsal and capture

Use **A1 → description → goal** for odd participant codes and **goal → A1 → description** for even codes. Then show review draft, saved draft and receipt in both routes. Switch the card to simulate successful verification only after the participant describes the next action. Ask when they expect to find the report in My cases, and separately when they expect a reviewer to see it. Score these as distinct answers.

A desk walkthrough identified three changes incorporated into the cards. Saving and submitting now have separate confirmations. An unassigned reviewer sees routing details and must claim the task before seeing the description. The deletion card acknowledges the request and explains that a decision is pending. These are copy and flow checks performed against the proposed brief. Measure comprehension, timing and device effects during actual sessions.

## Price and staff time

Use 20–30 hypothetical price interviews. Show free self-service in each variant. Compare KSh399 and KSh499 for the same candidate deliverable with presentation order balanced across interviews. Candidate scope is one identified company, one factual summary and approved request, one delivery follow-up and one response summary. Show the KSh999 evidence-pack hypothesis separately with one case and a defined evidence inventory and chronology. Counsel must clear exact service wording before offering it. Ask what buyers think is included, what they would do instead and what would make them decline. Record price choices as stated preferences.

Time synthetic work in separate buckets for screening, review, correction, follow-up and support. Keep abandoned and referred cases in the denominator. Under the [audit model](../audit/02_BUSINESS_AND_COSTS.md) at 20% conversion, KSh10 per minute, 8% payment/refund allowance and KSh41 per report in free work and acquisition/variable costs, break-even paid effort before fixed infrastructure is about **15.2 minutes at KSh399** and **24.4 minutes at KSh499**. Recalculate with observed effort and low-conversion scenarios before considering a paid pilot. Keep measured staff time, stated buying intent and eventual paid conversion as separate evidence.
