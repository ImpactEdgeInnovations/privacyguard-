# Phase 0 session plan and budget

The first round uses fictional cases with **12 usability sessions and 20 separate price interviews**. The proposed ceiling is **KSh53,000 for research sessions**. It is based on planning assumptions. Confirm the actual rate, participant allowances and availability before committing spend.

| Step | Work | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prepare | Assign a research facilitator and a fluent Kiswahili reviewer. Rehearse with the English and Kiswahili cards. Agree how to schedule sessions, notify participants and store or remove contact details and coded notes. | Approved script in both languages, rehearsal notes, participant notice and recruitment plan. |
| 2. Moderate 12 sessions | Schedule 45 minutes each. Start with six English-first and six Kiswahili-first sessions. Recruit across small-screen use and different levels of digital confidence. Counterbalance intake order within each language and run the same comprehension checks. | Coded first choices, timing, prompts, privacy comprehension and quotes stripped of identifying details. Record participant mix and missed sessions. |
| 3. Run 20 price interviews | Schedule 25 minutes each. Confirm candidate offer wording with the assigned advocate before presenting it. Vary which price participants see first. Include the free route in each interview. | Comprehension and stated choice for KSh399 and KSh499, reasons for declining, and differences by first price shown. |
| 4. Decide and retest | Score first-pass intake and case-action results. At 12 participants, the proposed 80% threshold requires 10 correct unaided responses. Inspect language results separately. Correct and retest any assumption that review sends a notice or that a fee buys an official outcome. | Summary with counts and denominators, copy revisions, retest scope and updated recommendation. |
