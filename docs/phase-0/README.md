# PrivacyGuard Kenya Phase 0 kickoff

**10 November 2026 · Validation underway**

The owner reviewed and approved the audit and asked to start. This record applies that approval to the first phase of the [revised roadmap](../audit/08_ROADMAP.md). The starting design is “Check a message”, with description-only intake and a case-first dashboard. The offer variants are research hypotheses. Budget, named owners and sign-offs remain open.

| Workstream | First deliverable | Status | Next input |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compliance and service scope | [Counsel and privacy brief](01_CLEARANCE_BRIEF.md) and [draft intake DPIA](04_INTAKE_DPIA_DRAFT.md) | Source follow-up and first assessment draft ready for review | Name a Kenyan advocate and a privacy lead. Confirm the operating entity and expected data flows. |
| Usability and unit economics | [Validation kit](02_VALIDATION_KIT.md), [Kiswahili draft cards](intake-test-cards-sw.html) and [session plan and budget](06_SESSION_PLAN_AND_BUDGET.md) | 12 usability sessions and 20 price interviews scoped at a proposed KSh53,000 ceiling | Confirm the research ceiling, name the facilitator and arrange Kiswahili review. |
| Phase 1 engineering | [Intake build brief](03_PHASE_1_BUILD_BRIEF.md) and [workflow tabletop](05_WORKFLOW_TABLETOP.md) | Six synthetic exception paths reviewed and incorporated into the candidate contract | Confirm scope, evidence of Phase 0 clearance and Phase 1 budget at the roadmap checkpoint. |

The first engineering milestone is one private report from verified account to assigned review, with an auditable export and deletion path. A description-only report must enter the same review queue as one with evidence. B02, B03, B08 and B09 supply the visual direction. The original boards and registers remain the dated audit record.

The Phase 0 exit decision needs a written scope opinion, privacy and processor decisions, approved audience and copy, observed usability and workload results, and a scoped Phase 1 instruction. Cost estimates and dates depend on those inputs. A later real-data pilot has its own release checkpoint.
