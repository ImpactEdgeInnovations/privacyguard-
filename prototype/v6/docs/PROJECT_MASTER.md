# Project master — PrivacyGuard Kenya

## Product promise
**"Unwanted messages? You have rights."** Help ordinary Kenyan consumers record unsolicited SMS/WhatsApp/email/call marketing, ask companies how their data was obtained and used, request that marketing stop, and where appropriate prepare a complaint to the ODPC and explore lawful remedies including **possible** compensation. No guaranteed award, no false regulatory affiliation.

## Target use
- Simple enough for a consumer familiar with WhatsApp and M-Pesa to use on an affordable Android phone.
- One clear call to action: **Upload a message** / **Check my message**.
- Public landing page before login: ODPC, possible compensation, benefits, how it works, free-to-start and optional assistance.
- Consumer dashboard after login: cases, next action, separate company and ODPC milestones, notifications, permissions.
- Operations dashboard: aggregate pipeline, exception queues, individual case trail, sent/inbound company and ODPC correspondence, delivery receipts, deadlines, evidence and authorisation review.

## Business model
- Official ODPC filing is free. PrivacyGuard is an independent paid convenience/administrative assistance service, with a free self-service route.
- Potential optional assisted service(s), initial indicative KSh 199 for limited admin actions; actual pricing, scope, taxes, refunds and Kenyan legal boundaries are **not yet approved**.
- No litigation success fee, ODPC award percentage or automatic paid upsell in MVP; any legally regulated service requires separate counsel review.
- Fees introduced only after initial free screening, clearly disclosed before purchase/processing starts.

## Core operating journey
1. Consumer uploads screenshot/evidence, gives minimal contact and accurate-report declaration; phone/email verification where needed.
2. Platform screens evidence; reviewer handles exceptions, verifies legal company and contact.
3. Consumer separately authorises a **specific** company request; admin-safe queued notice to verified address.
4. Monitor company response and statutory clocks; ingest written reply or record expiry, with actual delivery evidence.
5. Consumer reviews company outcome and chooses to close, seek clarification, or ask to proceed to ODPC.
6. If authorised, generate ODPC-compatible complaint/evidence pack; user reviews and confirms accuracy.
7. Consumer files through official ODPC portal, entering any ID information **directly there**. Never store National ID images in PrivacyGuard MVP.
8. Customer returns with ODPC reference / acknowledgement; record as unverified until matched to actual official communication.
9. Track regulator requests, determination, possible award, appeal and actual payment receipt as separate facts.

## Explicit non-goals
- No group claims until separate consent framework and legal design are approved.
- No bulk automatic ODPC filing, scraping, CAPTCHA bypass or fabricated case statuses.
- No AI legal verdicts, payout estimation/gamification or guarantee of compensation.
- No full phone inbox/SMS scanning or contact harvesting.
- No automatic acceptance of settlement terms.

## Primary interfaces
A. Public acquisition and trust landing. B. Consumer report and case tracker. C. Operations casework and correspondence command centre. D. Operator privacy/compliance controls.

## Working definition of success
An individual with limited tech literacy can understand the purpose and optional fees, upload evidence and monitor case progress; operators can verify case identity and send notices reliably; a complaint pack is reproducible, does not contain unneeded personal data and is submitted only through an approved process. No fake official states; no accidental duplicate messages; all permissions auditable.
