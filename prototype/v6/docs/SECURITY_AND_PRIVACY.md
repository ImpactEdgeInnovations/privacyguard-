# Privacy, protection and abuse prevention requirements

## Minimal collection
- Do not collect National ID/passport images or numbers in the ordinary PrivacyGuard MVP flow. Route users to the official ODPC portal for those details.
- Phone/email, names, messages and correspondence remain **personal data** even with permission; consent is not immunity from Kenyan data-protection law.
- Collect only case-relevant screenshots or exports; offer redaction guidance to exclude other people/irrelevant messages. Avoid phone-wide inbox access.
- Use a purpose/lawful-basis registry. Not all processing necessarily rests on consent; distinguish service processing notice from action-specific authority.
- Company contact approval, ODPC pack approval, legal representation authority and settlement authority are not interchangeable.

## Security and compliance gates
- Formal ODPC registration assessment for data-controller/processor role, statutory thresholds and applicability.
- Independent Kenyan law/privacy review, privacy policy, processors and vendors, retention periods, international transfers and DPIA as applicable.
- Data classification; encryption at rest and transit; private evidence access, signed URLs; operator MFA and granular case assignment.
- Virus scanning, MIME validation, bounded upload sizes, file quarantine, immutable original and reviewer working copies.
- User-access requests, correction, deletion/retention exceptions; documented expiry for abandoned drafts and temporary files.
- Backups, restoration tests, documented incident playbooks and breach reporting obligations.

## Anti-abuse controls
1. Phone/email proof of control; account takeover safeguards and rate limits.
2. Duplicate/reused screenshot fingerprinting and claim correlation; don't auto-condemn innocent users.
3. Source attribution checks: sender ID could be spoofed or belong to an aggregator.
4. Original message/export when available and user-confirmed chronology; a screenshot/hash alone cannot prove origin.
5. Evidence anomaly review queue; manual dispute or correction mechanism.
6. Verified company contact provenance and expiry; no random email guessing.
7. Human review for abusive/repetitive notices, settlements, legality and regulatory submissions.
8. Audit any send/approval and preserve consumer's permission version and scope.
9. Public outputs anonymised and aggregate only; no group discovery exposing individuals in MVP.
10. Inbound mail treated as untrusted content; scan attachments and block prompt injection/executable content.

## Claim and regulator safety
- ODPC filing is free; PrivacyGuard optional assistance may be chargeable with clear advance prices.
- ODPC identity requirements remain ODPC's, not waived by PrivacyGuard consent.
- Consumer-reported submission ≠ official acknowledgment; award ≠ paid compensation; company compliance ≠ closure.
- Document actual source/version for legal rules, not just hardcoded seven/fourteen day figures.

## Must confirm before launch
Detailed retention durations, ODPC registration category, whether foreign-hosted storage is permitted with safeguards, accepted form of representative authority, current filing requirements, available email/API channels and professional limits on fee-based legal-document assistance.
